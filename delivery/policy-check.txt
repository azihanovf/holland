1. GOOGLE — GAMBLING AND GAMES
URL: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15132179?hl=en-GB
Publication/update date: NOT SHOWN. English enforcement text was read.
The page has distinct offline, online and social-casino categories. Offline
promotion covers lawful physical gambling venues; it is forbidden where local
advertising law prohibits it. Netherlands is not in the illustrative prohibited
offline locations list, which does not establish this project's eligibility.
The Netherlands online operator entry requires a Dutch Gambling Authority
licence. That online table cannot be used as an offline certification shortcut.
The current general certification section requires current licence credentials,
evidence connecting advertiser/licensee/domain, a visible age warning, help,
terms, licensee details and a privacy policy naming the controller. Its application
tracks expressly name online gambling/promoting content and social/non-casino
games. The offline track/application applicability is therefore an unresolved
project condition: confirm it with Google Ads Support for this advertiser/domain.
Do not claim either a universal offline exemption or that an online certificate is
automatically required for every offline hotel. Social casino is simulated online
gambling without a valuable prize and is not this project. The page's September
2026 application change concerns social casino, not hotel accommodation.
